# Overview

The Krista Salesforce Customer Service Module Extension connects to the Salesforce system and its Customer Service module and performs various operations.

**Prerequisite**

* To connect to the Salesforce Customer Service module, you must have a Salesforce Developer account.

**Note:**

The Krista Salesforce Customer Service Module Extension:
* Supports only the entities such as Case, Contact, and Customer.
* Does not support any catalog requests.
